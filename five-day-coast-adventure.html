The 5-Day Diani Adventure

5 Days · Diani Coast Adventure

From the moment you land to the moment you leave — a journey we’ve already planned, built around the same four tides that shape everything we do.

This is the journey most first-time guests choose — and the one we recommend if you only take our advice once.

Five days, four tides. We take you from the airport to a stay that already feels like home, help you find your style for the coast, put you on the water aboard a traditional jahazi, take you to meet the people who make this coast what it is — and finish with a surprise, courtesy of Palm & Tide, before we see you off.

Your Journey

Five Days, Day by Day

  • Airport pickup and warm welcome
  • Check-in to your chosen stay — boutique hotel, cottage, villa or eco lodge
  • Home-prepared Swahili welcome meal
  • Evening to settle in and get your bearings
  • Visit local designers and independent studios
  • Find linen, kanga and tailoring built for the heat
  • Pick up handcrafted jewellery and accessories
  • Look local, act local — ready for the days ahead
  • Sail aboard a traditional jahazi or hand-carved ngalawa
  • Swim alongside dolphins in the channel
  • Snorkel the coral gardens
  • Swahili seafood lunch and a sunset cruise back
  • E-bike ride through Mijikenda’s villages and backroads
  • Tea with a local family, a walk through the market
  • Traditional Coatal Dances
  • The coast’s everyday rhythm, not a show for visitors
  • A surprise experience, on us — our way of saying thank you
  • Time for a final beach walk and last-minute souvenirs
  • Check-out and airport transfer
  • A journey that’s already planned for next time
