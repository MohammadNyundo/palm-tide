Meet the Coast

Two bike excursions that finish the journey with people — coastal villages and the forested hills above them.

Fourth Tide · Connection

Every journey should end with the people who make a place what it is. These two rides slow everything down, on purpose — you'll cover less ground than a car would, and see a great deal more.

01

Coastal Village Bike Excursion

Ride through the villages just inland from the beach strip, stopping for tea with a family, a look inside a working kitchen, and a walk through a local market — the everyday rhythm of the coast, not a performance of it.

  • Half-day
  • Village visits
  • Local guide
02

Shimba Hills Bike Excursion

Head inland and uphill into the forested reserve above Diani — sable antelope, colobus monkeys and a cooler, greener climate that feels like a different country entirely, all within an hour of the beach.

  • Full-day
  • Shimba Hills Reserve
  • Wildlife
